# Orbit X2 Smart Thermostat — User Manual

_Firmware 3.x. The Orbit X2 is a fictional product._

## 1. Installation

1. Switch off power to your heating system at the breaker.
2. Remove the old thermostat and label each wire (R, C, W, Y, G).
3. Mount the Orbit X2 base plate and connect the wires to the matching terminals.
   The **C (common) wire is required**; the Orbit X2 does not run on batteries.
4. Clip the display onto the base plate and switch the power back on.

## 2. First setup

Hold the dial for three seconds to open setup. Choose your language, connect to a
2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network (5 GHz networks are not supported), and select your heating type.
The default comfort temperature is 20.5 °C and the default eco temperature is 17 °C.

## 3. Schedules and eco mode

The Orbit X2 learns your schedule over the first **10 days**. Eco mode activates
automatically when no motion is detected for 2 hours, or when every paired phone has left
the home geofence (default radius: 500 m).

## 4. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | No power on the C wire | Check the C wire connection and the breaker. |
| E3 | Wi-Fi connection lost | Restart your router; the thermostat retries every 5 minutes. |
| E5 | Temperature sensor fault | Hold the dial for 20 seconds to reset. If it returns, contact support. |
| E7 | Heating system did not respond within 15 minutes | Check that the boiler is switched on and its pressure is between 1 and 2 bar. Then hold the dial and select **Settings > Heating > Re-detect system**. |
| E9 | Firmware update failed | Leave the device connected to Wi-Fi; it retries the update overnight. |

## 5. Maintenance

Clean the display with a dry cloth. Do not use spray cleaners. Firmware updates install
automatically between 02:00 and 04:00.

## 6. Warranty

The Orbit X2 comes with a **3-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects.
Damage from incorrect wiring is not covered.
