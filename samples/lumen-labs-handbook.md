# Lumen Labs Employee Handbook

_Version 4.2, effective January 2026. Lumen Labs is a fictional company._

## 1. Working hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your team's home time zone. Outside core
hours you can organise your day as you like, as long as you work an average of 38 hours a week.

## 2. Time off

### 2.1 Vacation

- New employees receive **24 vacation days** per calendar year, prorated in their first year.
- After three full years of service this increases to **27 days**.
- Up to **5 unused days carry over** into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.
- Vacation requests longer than five consecutive working days need approval at least
  four weeks in advance.

### 2.2 Sick leave

There is no fixed limit on sick days. For absences longer than three consecutive days,
please send a doctor's note to people@lumenlabs.example.

### 2.3 Parental leave

All parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to
three blocks within 18 months of the birth or adoption.

## 3. Remote work

Lumen Labs is remote-first. Every employee receives a one-time **home office budget of
EUR 1,200** in their first month and EUR 300 per year after that. Employees may work from
another country for up to **60 days per calendar year**, provided they inform People Ops
at least two weeks before travelling.

## 4. Expenses

- Submit expenses within **30 days** in the Ledgerly app, with a photo of the receipt.
- Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to EUR 45 per day.
- Train travel is preferred over flights for journeys under five hours.
- Purchases over EUR 500 need prior written approval from your manager.

## 5. Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of EUR 1,500 for courses, books and
conferences, plus three paid learning days per year.
